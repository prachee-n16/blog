This [[Operators|operator]] is
$$\hat{A} = \hat{A}^\dagger$$
where $\hat{A}^\dagger$ s the **transpose and complex conjugate** of $\hat{A}$.

The matrix representation of a general Hermitian operator is:
$$\hat{A} = \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ c & d \end{pmatrix}, \quad \hat{A}^\dagger = \begin{pmatrix} a^* & c^* \\ b^* & d^* \end{pmatrix}$$
a and d are **real numbers**; b and c are **complex conjugates** of each other.

Note
- Physical observables (eigenvalues) of a Hermitian operator are real. 
- The eigenvectors of a Hermitian matrix comprise a complete set of basis states
